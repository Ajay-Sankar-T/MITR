MITR

  1. Home
  2. MITR

MITR is a body of faculty and student MITRs. They offer peer counselling and support according to students' needs. The motto of MITR is that no one on campus should be left unattended in emotional distress.

All MITRs are trained in basic counselling skills and empathy. They are available in almost all departments and hostels. Regardless of department or hostel, any student can contact any MITR.

Introduction to MITR

Prof. Sathyanarayana N Gummadi

Dean of Students (DoSt)

Programs

MITR provides peer counselling to students in distress and collaborates with different agencies in the IITM community. MITR organizes Barefoot Counselling training for new MITRs in collaboration with the Wellness Centre. They also conduct surveys and events for students based on specific needs.

READ RECENT EVENTS

Meet the MITR

MITR is guided by a faculty advisor. At present, Prof. Sarith P. Sathian is the advisor to MITR.

Almost every department has a faculty MITR. Students can contact their faculty MITR for any distress they may be going through.

Student MITRs are available in every hostel and in most departments. They undergo Barefoot Counselling training and work in coordination with the Wellness Centre.

MITR students are recruited every year by the preceding year's MITR team through interviews. To know more about the recruitment process, please follow the updates shared by the MITR team.
