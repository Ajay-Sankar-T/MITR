About MITR

Peer support, trained and always nearby

All MITRs are trained in basic counselling skills and empathy, and are available in almost every department and hostel. Regardless of department or hostel, any student can reach out to any MITR.

MITR provides peer counselling to students in distress and collaborates with agencies across the IITM community, running Barefoot Counselling training for new MITRs with the Wellness Centre, along with surveys and events based on student needs.

Introduction to MITR

Led from the Dean's office

MITR operates under the guidance of the Office of the Dean of Students, ensuring peer support stays connected to the institute's broader wellness efforts.

Prof. Sathyanarayana N Gummadi

Meet the MITR

Guided by faculty, run by students

MITR is guided by a faculty advisor — at present, Prof. Sarith P. Sathian. Almost every department has a faculty MITR, who students can contact for any distress they may be going through.

Student MITRs, present in every hostel and most departments, undergo Barefoot Counselling training and work in coordination with the Wellness Centre. They're recruited each year by the preceding year's MITR team through interviews — follow the MITR team's updates to know more.
